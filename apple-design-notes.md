# Apple.com Design System Analysis

## Typography

Apple uses their proprietary SF Pro font family, which consists of SF Pro Display for headlines and SF Pro Text for body copy. For web fallbacks, they use a system font stack that prioritizes native fonts on each platform. The typography is characterized by clean, lightweight fonts with excellent readability. Headlines use thin to regular weights, while body text uses regular weight for optimal legibility.

The font stack Apple uses is: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "SF Pro Display", "SF Pro Text", "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.

## Color Palette

Apple's color scheme is remarkably restrained. The primary palette consists of pure white (#FFFFFF) for backgrounds, pure black (#000000) or near-black (#1d1d1f) for text, and a carefully selected blue (#0071e3) for primary call-to-action buttons. Secondary text uses a medium gray (#86868b) for less prominent information.

Product sections often use soft, muted background colors like light blue (#f5f5f7), light gray, or white to create visual separation without harsh contrasts.

## Button Styles

Primary buttons use a solid blue (#0071e3) background with white text, featuring generous padding (12px 22px), rounded corners (980px border-radius for pill shape), and no border. On hover, the blue darkens slightly to #0077ed.

Secondary buttons use a white or transparent background with a thin border (#d2d2d7), black text, and the same pill shape. On hover, they may add a subtle background color.

Link-style buttons are simple text links in blue (#0071e3) that underline on hover.

## Spacing and Layout

Apple uses generous whitespace throughout. Section padding is typically 80-120px vertically. Content is centered with a max-width container. Product sections are full-width with centered content. Grid gaps are substantial (20-40px).

## Transitions and Animations

Apple uses subtle, smooth transitions with a duration of 0.3-0.4 seconds and an ease-out timing function. Hover effects are understated, typically just color changes. Scroll-triggered animations fade and slide content into view. The overall feel is refined and never jarring.

## Key Design Principles

1. Minimalism: Remove everything unnecessary
2. Whitespace: Let content breathe
3. Typography hierarchy: Clear distinction between headlines, subheadlines, and body
4. Subtle interactions: Hover states that feel natural
5. High-quality imagery: Products are the hero
6. Consistency: Same patterns repeated throughout
