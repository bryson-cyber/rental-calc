# AirDNA Data Sources Research

## Where AirDNA Gets Their Data

Based on official AirDNA documentation:

1. **Primary Sources:**
   - Airbnb (100% of listings monitored daily)
   - Vrbo/HomeAway (100% of listings monitored daily)
   - Booking.com (100% of listings monitored daily)

2. **Data Collection Method:**
   - Public listing data scraped daily
   - Daily pricing and calendar availability
   - Reviews and ratings
   - Property details (beds, baths, amenities)

3. **Additional Sources:**
   - Private hosts who connect their accounts
   - Short-term rental managers who share data
   - Over 10 million properties tracked globally

## Attribution Options (Without Mentioning AirDNA)

Instead of "Data powered by AirDNA", we can say:

**Option 1 (Recommended):**
"Data aggregated from Airbnb, Vrbo, and Booking.com listings"

**Option 2:**
"Real performance data from over 10 million vacation rentals"

**Option 3:**
"Market data from active Airbnb and Vrbo listings"

**Option 4 (Simplest):**
"Powered by Coach Inayah" (no data source mention at all)

## Recommendation

Since the user wants "Powered by Coach Inayah" branding, we should:
1. Remove all AirDNA references
2. Use "Powered by Coach Inayah" as the main attribution
3. Optionally add small text: "Data from Airbnb & Vrbo listings"
