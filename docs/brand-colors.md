# Coach Inayah Brand Colors (from masterclass.coachinayah.com)

## Primary Colors Identified

Based on the masterclass website:

1. **Gold/Mustard Yellow** - Primary accent color for buttons, highlights, headings
   - Hex: approximately #D4A84B or #C9A43E (warm gold/mustard)
   - Used for: CTA buttons, "WITHOUT" highlight, checkmarks, icons

2. **Light Pink/Blush Background** - Soft gradient background
   - Hex: approximately #F8E8E8 to #FDF5F5 (very light pink/blush)
   - Used for: Page background, creates warm welcoming feel

3. **White** - Card backgrounds
   - Hex: #FFFFFF
   - Used for: Content cards, clean sections

4. **Dark Text** - Headlines and body
   - Hex: approximately #1A1A1A or #2D2D2D (near black)
   - Used for: All text content

5. **Teal/Cyan** - Secondary accent (button gradient)
   - Hex: approximately #4ECDC4 or #5DD3CB
   - Used for: Button gradient effect (gold to teal)

## Color Scheme Summary

The Coach Inayah brand uses:
- **Warm, inviting palette** - soft pink backgrounds with gold accents
- **Professional but approachable** - not corporate, feels friendly
- **High contrast CTAs** - gold/teal buttons stand out on light backgrounds
- **Clean white cards** - content is easy to read

## Recommended Implementation

For the Rental Calculator:
- Background: Light pink/blush (#FDF5F5 or similar)
- Primary accent: Gold (#D4A84B)
- Secondary accent: Teal (#4ECDC4) for gradients
- Cards: White with subtle shadows
- Text: Dark charcoal (#1A1A1A)
- Success states: Teal
- Highlights: Gold

## Typography
- Serif font for headlines (elegant, professional)
- Sans-serif for body text (readable, modern)
