# Startup Investment Breakdown (Reference - Not for Initial Report)

## Acquisition Costs
| Line Item | Typical Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Security deposit | $1,500–$4,000 | Often 1 month rent; negotiable |
| First month rent | $1,500–$3,500 | Based on market and unit size |
| Application/lease fees | $50–$300 | Background/administrative fees |

## Design & Setup (CapEx)
| Line Item | Typical Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Furniture & décor | $10,000-$15,000 | Beds, sofa, dining, rugs, art, lamps |
| Appliances/smallwares | $800-$1500 | Coffee maker, toaster, cookware, utensils |
| Linens & bedding | $400–$900 | Sheets, duvets, pillows, mattress protectors, pillow protectors (1-3 sets/bed) |
| Smart tech (locks, hubs) | $300–$600 | Smart locks, Ring cameras |
| Labor costs | $2500-$6000 | Dependent on property size and design |

## Operations (OpEx)
| Line Item | Typical Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Professional cleaning setup | $150–$300 | Initial deep clean; then per-turn costs |
| Starter consumables | $400-$600 | Soaps, paper goods, coffee/tea, etc. |
| Photography | $275–$475 | Essential for conversion |
| Insurance | $30–$100/mo | STR liability/contents coverage |
| Software & automations | $30–$150/mo | Channel mgr, pricing, messaging |

## Program
| Line Item | Typical Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Turnkey Program Fee | $5,200.00 | Per property—startup support and education |

## Contingency
| Line Item | Typical Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| Reserve | Equivalent to 3 months expenses (rent/utilities) | Expenses during the 6-8 week build out |
