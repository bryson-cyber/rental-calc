# Market Report Format Analysis

## Key Insight: The Report Structure

The example market report has a specific format that works:

### Page 1-2: Executive Summary with Interpretation
Each metric is presented as:
- **Metric Name (Value):** Plain English explanation of what it means

Examples:
- "Avg. Monthly Listings (49.33):" → "There are just under 50 active listings monthly. With a small supply, performance can change a lot since the market depends on just a few listings."
- "Listing % Change (25%):" → "Listings increased by 25%, showing more properties are being added. This may mean more competition, but could also mean the area is becoming more popular."

### Key Metrics Covered:
1. **Market Listing Analysis**
   - Avg Monthly Listings
   - Listing % Change (YoY)
   - % Available Full Time
   - % Still Available from Last Year
   - % Professional hosts

2. **Availability Metrics**
   - What % Available Full Time means
   - Impact on market dynamics

3. **Professional vs Supplemental**
   - % Professional (21+ listings)
   - What it means for competition

4. **Occupancy Metrics**
   - Annual Occupancy
   - Occupancy % Change
   - Occupancy in days

5. **Financial Performance**
   - Annual ADR
   - Annual RevPAR
   - RevPAR % Change
   - Total average revenue

### The Pattern: Data + "What This Means"
Every single metric has:
1. The number
2. Plain English interpretation
3. What it means for the HOST/INVESTOR

## For Property Reports - The Vision

User inputs: **Address + Rent + Beds + Baths**

Output should include:
1. Property revenue estimate (filtered to same bed/bath)
2. Market health (all the metrics above, interpreted)
3. Competitors (same bed/bath, with amenity comparison)
4. Amenity gap analysis (what comps have that this property doesn't)
5. Seasonality breakdown
6. Startup costs estimate
7. Break-even timeline
8. GO/NO-GO verdict

All in ONE output from ONE input.
