# User Pain Points with STR Analysis Tools

## Source: Trustpilot AirDNA Reviews (1-star filtered)

### Pain Point 1: Data Accuracy Issues
**Quote:** "all the data is inaccurate and made up. Many areas and properties are stale and bad data."
**Quote:** "a lot of the data was not accurate and the filters didn't work properly"

**Opportunity:** Provide transparency about data sources, show "last updated" dates, and explain methodology

### Pain Point 2: Feature Discovery / Hidden Features
**Quote:** "Doesn't show you all the features you get on pro so I bought it and cancelled day of"

**Opportunity:** Clear feature comparison, free trial with full access, upfront transparency

### Pain Point 3: Billing/Subscription Issues
**Quote:** "If you cancel your subscription through their website they still charge you and will not refund"
**Quote:** "charged me for another month and they wouldn't even refund the second month"

**Opportunity:** Our tool is FREE - no subscription needed. This is a HUGE differentiator.

### Pain Point 4: Complexity / Learning Curve
From other reviews: Users mention needing training sessions with AirDNA staff

**Opportunity:** Make our tool so simple that no training is needed. Beginner-friendly by default.

### Pain Point 5: Lack of Downloadable Reports
**Quote (from positive review):** "I'd say they are lacking in downloadable reports - like PriceLabs lets you download the entire dataset in one table whereas airdna doesn't"

**Opportunity:** Easy PDF export, shareable reports, data downloads

## Key Competitive Advantages for Our Super App

1. **FREE** - No subscription, no hidden fees
2. **Simple** - No learning curve, beginner-friendly
3. **Transparent** - Show data sources and methodology
4. **Actionable** - Don't just show data, tell them what to DO
5. **Done-For-You Option** - If it's too complex, we'll do it for them
6. **AI-Powered Insights** - Not just data, but intelligent analysis
7. **Comparison Tools** - Side-by-side property comparisons
8. **Distance Context** - Show how far competitors are
9. **Profitability Focus** - Not just revenue, but actual profit potential
10. **Educational** - Teach them while showing them data

## Super App Feature Ideas Based on Pain Points
