## Super App AI Features to Build

### Tier 1: Quick Wins (Easy to Implement)

1. **AI Property Advisor Chat**
   - Natural language Q&A about any property or market
   - "Is this a good investment?" → Detailed analysis
   - "What should I charge?" → Pricing recommendation

2. **Smart Comparison Generator**
   - "Compare these 3 properties" → Side-by-side analysis
   - AI highlights key differences and winner

3. **Investment Score with Reasoning**
   - 1-100 score with detailed explanation
   - Pros/cons list generated automatically

4. **Amenity Recommendations**
   - Based on top performers, suggest what to add
   - "Top winners have hot tubs - you should add one"

### Tier 2: Medium Effort (Differentiation)

5. **Competitor Intelligence Report**
   - Analyze competitor listings via URL
   - Extract their pricing strategy
   - Identify gaps in the market

6. **Seasonal Strategy Generator**
   - Month-by-month pricing recommendations
   - Event calendar integration
   - Minimum stay suggestions

7. **Listing Description Writer**
   - Generate optimized Airbnb descriptions
   - Based on top performer analysis
   - SEO-optimized for Airbnb search

8. **Guest Persona Analysis**
   - Who books in this area?
   - What do they want?
   - How to target them?

### Tier 3: Advanced (Major Differentiation)

9. **Predictive Revenue Modeling**
   - Monte Carlo simulation for revenue ranges
   - Confidence intervals
   - Best/worst case scenarios

10. **Market Timing Advisor**
    - When to buy in this market
    - Supply/demand trend analysis
    - "Buy now" vs "Wait" recommendation

11. **Regulatory Risk Assessment**
    - Research local STR laws
    - Permit requirements
    - Risk score for regulation changes

12. **Portfolio Optimizer**
    - For users with multiple properties
    - Diversification recommendations
    - Cross-market analysis
