## Chapter 1: What is Airbnb Arbitrage?
**Read time: 3 min**

The arbitrage business model explained simply: You rent a property from a landlord, then list it on Airbnb/VRBO and keep the profit difference. No down payment. No mortgage. No property ownership required.

**Key topics:**
- How arbitrage differs from traditional real estate investing
- Why landlords agree to this arrangement
- The math: Monthly rent vs. short-term rental income
- Real examples of arbitrage profits
