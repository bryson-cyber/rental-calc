Based on the skill guide (SKILL.md), Step 3 is the gold standard. This assessment evaluates Step 1 against the same quality checklist.

## Quality Checklist Assessment

### 1. Guiding Questions for Each Section

| Section | Has Guiding Question? | Current State |
|---------|----------------------|---------------|
| Market Verdict Card | ✅ Yes | "What does this mean?" explanation included |
| Quick Insights | ❌ No | Section title only, no guiding question |
| Submarket Comparison | ✅ Yes | "Where in [City]?" as section title |

| Revenue Distribution | ❌ No | "What hosts actually earn" is descriptive, not a question |
| Guest Behavior Insights | ❌ No | "How guests book in this market" is descriptive |
| Competition Landscape | ❌ No | "Who you're competing against" is descriptive |
| Revenue by Property Type | ❌ No | "See what's working in this market" is descriptive |
| Market Performance Over Time | ❌ No | No guiding question |
| Comp Data Table | ❌ No | No guiding question |

**Score: 2/10 sections have true guiding questions**

**Recommendation:** Convert descriptive subtitles to questions:
- Quick Insights → "What should I know first about this market?"
- Market Health Score → "Is this market healthy for investors?"
- Revenue Distribution → "How much can I realistically expect to earn?"
- Guest Behavior → "How do guests typically book here?"
- Competition → "How competitive is this market?"
- Revenue by Property Type → "Which property types earn the most?"
- Historical Trends → "Is this market growing or declining?"
- Comp Data → "What are successful properties doing?"

### Overall Grade: B-

Step 1 has made significant progress with the Market Verdict Card, tooltips, and visual design. However, it still falls short of Step 3's quality in these key areas:

1. **Missing Guiding Questions** - Most sections use descriptive subtitles instead of questions
2. **Missing Contextual Comparisons** - No national benchmarks or market-to-market comparisons
3. **Missing Verdicts** - Many sections show data without interpretation
4. **Limited Letter Grades** - Only Market Verdict uses grades

---

## Priority Improvements

### High Priority (Match Step 3 Quality)

1. **Add guiding questions to all sections** - Convert "Revenue Distribution" to "How much can I realistically expect to earn?"

2. **Add national benchmark comparisons** - Show how this market compares to US averages for key metrics

3. **Add section verdicts** - Each section should end with a clear takeaway

4. **Add letter grades to more sections** - Market Health Score, Submarkets, Property Types

### Medium Priority

5. **Improve Revenue Distribution labels** - Use "Budget/Typical/Premium Earners" instead of percentiles

6. **Add confidence indicators** - Show sample sizes and data dates

7. **Add missing tooltips** - Guest Behavior, Competition metrics

### Lower Priority

8. **Add "What Should I Do Next?" CTA** - Guide users to Step 2 or Step 3 based on market grade

9. **Add Market Summary PDF export** - Let users save/share their market research

10. **Add submarket breakdown** - Currently returns 0 submarkets due to API limitations (requires alternative data source)

## Comparison to Step 3

### What Step 3 Does Well (That Step 1 Should Copy)

1. **Every section starts with a question** - "How does your rent compare?" not "Rent Validation"

2. **Clear verdicts with color coding** - "This Property Cash Flows" in green, not just numbers

3. **Contextual comparisons everywhere** - "261% more than long-term rental" not just "$103,378"

4. **Progressive disclosure** - Summary first, details on expand

5. **Confidence notes** - "Based on 26 similar properties | Avg. 0.4 mi away"

### What Step 1 Does Well

1. **Market Verdict Card** - Excellent implementation with letter grade and plain English

2. **Tooltips on key metrics** - Good coverage of main metrics

3. **Visual design** - Clean, modern, consistent styling

4. **Quick Insights** - Good summary of key takeaways

5. **"Uncommon in this market" messaging** - Good handling of limited data scenarios

---

## Conclusion

Step 1 is approximately **70% of the way** to matching Step 3's quality. The main gaps are:

1. Guiding questions (easy fix - just reword section titles)
2. National benchmarks (medium effort - need to add comparison data)
3. Section verdicts (medium effort - need to add interpretation logic)
4. More letter grades (easy fix - convert numeric scores to grades)

With these improvements, Step 1 would fully match Step 3's beginner-friendly approach.
