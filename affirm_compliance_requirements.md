# Affirm Website Compliance Checklist

Mandatory website requirements for merchants offering Affirm via Lumino

## 1. Refund & Return Policy (REQUIRED)
- A dedicated Refund / Return Policy page is live and publicly accessible
- Policy is linked in the website footer and visible before checkout
- Policy clearly states: eligibility, time window, refund method, and refund timing
- No vague, hidden, or conditional refund language

## 2. Offer & Service Clarity
- Clear description of products or services being sold
- Deliverables, scope, and fulfillment timing are explicitly stated
- No bundling of speculative future outcomes or undefined benefits
- High-ticket offers clearly explain what the customer receives

## 3. Marketing Claims & Testimonials
- No income, performance, approval, or outcome guarantees
- No investment, ROI, or 'risk-free' language
- Testimonials reflect typical results and real customer experiences
- Testimonials do not promise outcomes or exaggerate results

## 4. Checkout & Customer Transparency
- Total price is clearly displayed before payment
- Customer understands exactly what they are purchasing
- Affirm modal is unmodified and presented at checkout
- Key disclosures are not hidden post-purchase

## 5. Support & Contact Information
- Visible customer support section or contact page
- Valid support email or ticketing method is listed
- Expected response timeframe is reasonable and clear
- Support information matches the business entity

## 6. Business Identity & Trust Signals
- Legal business name matches Affirm application
- Physical or registered business address is visible
- Website has no broken links or placeholder content

## 7. Acknowledgement & Readiness
- Merchant understands Affirm controls underwriting and loan decisions
- Merchant agrees to ongoing monitoring and performance reviews
- Website content is maintained post-launch
