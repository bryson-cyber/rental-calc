## Rabbu (rabbu.com/airbnb-calculator)

**Tagline:** "Estimate Airbnb rental revenue for any address in the United States"

**Key Features:**
1. **Detailed Revenue Projections** - Historic + current Airbnb data
2. **Seasonality and Market Trends** - Seasonal impact visualization
3. **Precision Comp Selection** - Filter and select comparable properties
4. **Custom Tailored Analysis** - Input specific property filters, comps, expenses
5. **User-Centric Design** - Intuitive interface
6. **US-Wide Coverage** - Data for any US market

**Data Points Provided:**
- Average Daily Rate (ADR) - next 30 days
- Occupancy - next 30 days
- RevPAN (Revenue Per Available Night) - ADR x Occupancy
- Seasonalized Monthly Revenue Projection - with 25%/75% percentile chart
- Nearby Comparable Properties - with calendar view for occupancy/pricing

**Unique Angles:**
- **Weekly data refresh** (we should highlight our data freshness)
- **Comp calendar view** - see actual booking calendars of comps
- **25%/75% percentile projections** - range of outcomes
- **ROI underwriting calculator** - Cap Rate, Cash-on-Cash, Gross Yield

**What They Do That We Don't:**
1. ❌ Cap Rate calculation
2. ❌ Cash-on-Cash return
3. ❌ Gross Yield
4. ❌ Comp calendar view (see actual bookings)
5. ❌ 25%/75% percentile revenue ranges
6. ❌ "Browse Airbnbs for sale" - properties already underwritten

## BNB Calc (bnbcalc.com)

**Tagline:** "Reveal your property's rental profitability"

**Analysis Types:**
1. **Buy** - Purchase property and list on Airbnb
2. **Own** - List a property you already own
3. **Rent** - Rent from landlord and list (ARBITRAGE - our use case!)
4. **Cohost** - Manage property for owner

**Key Features:**
- Instant revenue projections from Airbnb AND Vrbo
- 40 short-term AND long-term comps with revenues
- Financial summary with expenses and financing
- **Estimated tax deductions** (HUGE differentiator)
- Share link and printout
- Import links from Zillow or MLS
- Multi-currency support (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, etc.)

**Unique Angles:**
1. **Tax Deduction Calculator** - 100% bonus depreciation, cost segregation
2. **Section 8 Map** - For long-term rental investors
3. **Lead Form for Agents** - White-label embed
4. **Zillow/MLS Import** - Paste link to auto-populate
5. **Printable Reports** - Shareable analysis

**What They Do That We Don't:**
1. ❌ Tax deduction estimates (bonus depreciation, cost segregation)
2. ❌ Long-term rental comps alongside STR comps
3. ❌ Zillow/MLS link import
4. ❌ Printable/shareable reports
5. ❌ Cohost analysis mode
6. ❌ Multi-currency support
7. ❌ "Rent" mode specifically for arbitrage (we have this but could highlight more)

**Pricing:**
- Free tier available
- Paid plans for more features

# GAP ANALYSIS - What We're Missing

## HIGH PRIORITY (Differentiators we should add):

### 1. **ROI Metrics** (All competitors have this)
- Cap Rate calculation
- Cash-on-Cash return
- Gross Yield
- **Why it matters:** Investors speak this language. Without it, we look amateur.

### 2. **Tax Deduction Estimates** (BNB Calc unique)
- Bonus depreciation calculator
- Cost segregation analysis
- **Why it matters:** HUGE value-add for investors. Tax savings can make or break a deal.

### 3. **Airbnb vs Long-Term Rental Comparison** (Mashvisor)
- Side-by-side comparison of STR vs LTR returns
- **Why it matters:** Helps investors decide if arbitrage is better than traditional rental.

### 4. **Printable/Shareable Reports** (BNB Calc, Rabbu)
- PDF export
- Share link
- **Why it matters:** Users need to share with partners, lenders, landlords.

### 5. **25%/75% Percentile Projections** (Rabbu)
- Show range of outcomes, not just average
- **Why it matters:** Sets realistic expectations, builds trust.

## MEDIUM PRIORITY (Nice to have):

### 6. **Comp Calendar View** (Rabbu)
- See actual booking calendars of comparable properties
- **Why it matters:** Shows real demand patterns.

### 7. **Setup Costs Breakdown** (BNB Calc, Mashvisor)
- Furniture, supplies, photography, etc.
- **Why it matters:** Helps calculate true startup investment.

### 8. **Guest Origin Data** (Airbtics)
- Where guests come from
- **Why it matters:** Helps with marketing targeting.

### 9. **Booking Lead Time** (Airbtics)
- How far in advance guests book
- **Why it matters:** Helps with pricing strategy.

### 10. **Regulation Data** (Airbtics)
- STR regulations by market
- **Why it matters:** Critical for compliance.

## LOW PRIORITY (Future considerations):

- Multi-currency support
- VRBO/Booking.com data
- Draw custom market boundaries
- Heatmaps
- International markets
- Dynamic pricing tool
- Property finder
